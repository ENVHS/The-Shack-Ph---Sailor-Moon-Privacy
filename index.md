# Sailor Moon Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Sailor Moon is a Discord bot that provides moderation, member onboarding, AI chat, leveling and economy features, games, roles, and ticket-management functionality.

## Information Sailor Moon Processes

Depending on which features are enabled in a Discord server, Sailor Moon may process:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server and channel IDs
- Usernames, display names, roles, and other Discord member information
- Message content when required for moderation, AI chat, games, leveling, or other message-based features
- Moderation records such as warnings and related actions
- Leveling, XP, and economy data
- Ticket information
- AI conversation history and related context

## How Information Is Used

Information is processed only to provide Sailor Moon's features, including:

- Moderating Discord servers
- Responding to users through AI-powered chat features
- Tracking server participation for leveling and economy features
- Managing member onboarding, roles, and welcome features
- Operating ticket and support features
- Providing games and other community features

## AI Features

When users interact with Sailor Moon's AI features, relevant message content and conversation context may be sent to third-party AI service providers in order to generate responses.

Only information required to provide the requested AI functionality is sent.

## Data Storage

Sailor Moon may store information needed for its features, including moderation records, XP and economy data, ticket information, and AI conversation history.

Stored information is used only to operate and maintain the bot and its features.

## Data Sharing

Sailor Moon does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when required to operate certain bot features, such as AI response generation.

## Data Removal

Users and Discord server administrators may request removal of data associated with them or their server.

To request deletion or ask a privacy-related question, contact the Sailor Moon bot administrator through the Discord server where Sailor Moon is available.

## Discord

Sailor Moon operates through the Discord platform. Discord's own collection and processing of information is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Sailor Moon's features change. The latest version will always be available at this page.
